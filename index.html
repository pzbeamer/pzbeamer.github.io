About

I study how people learn word meanings from limited evidence. My lab combines behavioral experiments, computational models, and large-scale corpus analysis to ask how children and adults build vocabularies so quickly.

Before joining Example University in 2023, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the Institute for Language Research. I completed my PhD at State University, advised by Prof. Alex Smith.

Prospective students: I am recruiting PhD students for Fall 2027. Please apply through the department and mention my name in your statement.
